# Code of Conduct

On the Fly is an open-source project. We want it to be a respectful, constructive, and welcoming place to report bugs, ask questions, review code, and contribute.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and professional.
- Assume good intent; disagree on technical merits, not people.
- Give actionable feedback in reviews and issues.
- Help newcomers when you can.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Trolling, spam, or repeated bad-faith arguments
- Publishing others’ private information without permission
- Any conduct that would make reasonable contributors feel unsafe or unwelcome

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject issues, comments, or pull requests that violate this code. Repeated or serious violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.
